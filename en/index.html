Frequently asked questions about pilates in Valencia

How many pilates studios are there in Valencia?

The directory lists 38 pilates studios in Valencia city and the surrounding metropolitan area, including municipalities such as Paterna, Torrent, L'Eliana and Aldaia.

How much does pilates cost in Valencia?

Drop-in classes range from €10 to €49 per session. Monthly memberships for 2 sessions per week cost between €78 and €155 depending on the studio and format (reformer or mat).

What is the difference between reformer pilates and mat pilates?

Reformer pilates uses a spring-resistance machine that works across multiple planes of movement. Mat pilates uses only body weight. Reformer typically costs 30–60 % more than mat due to the equipment and smaller class sizes.

Are there pilates studios in Valencia with physiotherapy?

Yes, several centres in the directory combine pilates with clinical physiotherapy — ideal for rehabilitation, injuries, chronic pain or pelvic floor work. Filter them in the physiotherapy section.

Can I do pilates as a complete beginner?

Yes. Most studios in the directory offer classes for all levels, including absolute beginners. Many carry out an initial assessment to adapt the exercises to your level and needs.
